How FreshPulse Dynamic Pricing Works

Algorithmic waste prevention and consumer surplus optimization

FreshPulse implements a continuous decay function modeled after financial options pricing and demand-elasticity curves. Rather than writing off unsold stock at a 100% loss, our platform progressively drops prices to meet market clearing demand before expiration.
